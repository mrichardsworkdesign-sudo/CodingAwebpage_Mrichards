My design process begins with curiosity, research, and a desire to understand the purpose behind each project. I take time to explore the client’s goals, audience, and visual direction before developing ideas. Through research, gathering inspiration, sketching, and experimenting with typography, color, and layout, I work to find a visual solution that is both meaningful and functional. I see each project as an opportunity to explore new ideas, solve problems, and communicate a message through design. As my ideas develop, I refine and revise my work, paying attention to the details that bring everything together. My goal is to create thoughtful designs that connect with people, communicate with purpose, and leave a lasting impression.

Chai Essence

Brand identity · Product design

Chai Essence is a tea brand I built from concept to product. I began by researching traditional Chinese tea culture, including its history and its varieties. I used that research to shape a complete brand identity: a logo, a visual system, and brand guidelines for consistent use across materials. I then carried the identity into product design, creating a tea set and a line of tea containers that form one cohesive product range.

North Central Markets Postcard

Print design · Sold on campus

I designed a postcard sold at North Central Markets, a campus market event featuring handmade products. My postcard was produced and sold through the Design Agency vendor, alongside work from other designers. The project took a design from concept through to a finished printed product, made for real customers.

Field Museum Poster

Personal project · Poster design

This was a personal project: a poster I designed for the Field Museum in Chicago, made on my own initiative rather than for the museum. I chose it as a way to practice layout and poster design on a real subject, with the same constraints a working designer would face. A museum poster has to catch attention from a distance, communicate key information quickly, and still reflect the character of the institution.
