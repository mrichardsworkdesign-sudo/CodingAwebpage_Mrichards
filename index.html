About Me

Bio and Identity

Meredith Richards is a Graphic Design major at North Central College (NCC) in Naperville, Illinois, where she is pursuing her Bachelor of Fine Arts and expects to graduate in 2028. She holds NCC's Graphic Design Scholarship, which she has received since 2025, and was awarded the college's Studio Art Scholarship in 2024. Before arriving at North Central, she received the Tommie Adams Award at Naperville North High School. With a strong foundation in both fine art and design, Meredith brings technical skill and an artist's eye to every project she takes on.

Meredith joined the North Central College Design Agency to gain firsthand experience in the collaborative design process and to learn how to manage client relationships from concept to completion. Through the Agency, she has collaborated with the NCC Library to create logos and visual resources for a range of campus organizations. Working with these groups as clients, she applied branding and visual communication principles to help each organization present a clear and recognizable identity, and she incorporated client feedback at every stage of the process. The work strengthened her skills in brand development and showed her how much good design depends on listening closely to the people it serves.

Also through the Agency, Meredith partnered with NCC First-Gen to design a display highlighting the experiences and identities of first-generation college students. Working alongside campus partners, she created layouts and visual elements that communicated the organization's mission and gave its community a visible presence on campus. The project challenged her to turn real stories and experiences into a cohesive visual message, a task that called for both care and strong compositional judgment.

Beyond campus, Meredith has volunteered as a designer for Peace Home Church since August 2025, creating promotional and visual materials for church events and activities. These projects have allowed her to support communication and community engagement while applying design principles to produce content that is clear, inviting, and visually appealing.

Her toolkit includes Adobe Illustrator, Photoshop, InDesign, and After Effects, along with Figma and HTML/CSS, giving her the range to work across print, digital, and motion. Her areas of focus include visual identity design, branding, typography, layout and editorial design, print design, and motion graphics. This breadth lets her approach each project with the format and medium that best fits the client's goals.

Meredith draws inspiration from her father, who works in marketing and has guided her creative journey, motivating her to continually refine her craft and strive for excellence in the field. Outside of design, she enjoys painting, drawing, and photography, which allow her to explore different artistic techniques and express her creativity across various mediums. These hands-on practices continue to shape the way she thinks about composition, color, and form in her design work.

About the Project

This website was created by [Your Name] as a student project for ARTD 245: Web Design at North Central College in Naperville, IL, in 2026. All original graphics were created using Photoshop, Illustrator, or Procreate, and the site was hand-coded using HTML and CSS in Dreamweaver. Text content and design theory are adapted from Flux Academy, Tilda Education, and Dribbble articles.
